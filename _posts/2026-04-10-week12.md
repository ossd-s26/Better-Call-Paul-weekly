## Role of OSS in Industry

From the discussion on the usage of OSS in industry, I realized that when working, I was actually interfacing with a lot of OSS but I had just assumed that it was closed source as they felt like great products. I suppose that is my bias against OSS as I just think that logically if the software is good then it makes sense to close source it and sell it. It was also useful getting a sense for how foundational OSS is to the modern software industry as numerous companies build critical infra on top of them as opposed to building everything in house. I found the stat that OSS contributes trillions to the US economy as companies don't have to build this software in house and can instead focus on specializing on their own product. 

## Thoughts on PyTorch

I knew that PyTorch was originally developed by Facebook but wasn't aware that it had transitioned to the PyTorch foundation that was under the Linux foundation. It is interesting how despite it being that useful and critical to the ml ecosystem, the community still had the power and incentive to transition it to ensure vendor neutrality. I suppose that this demonstrates the enduring power of OSS as even large business actors want it to be neutral. Or there is at least a strong enough community force to prevent any inkling of non-neutrality.
