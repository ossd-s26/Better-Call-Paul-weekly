## Progress on Frosty

This week I worked on adding functionality to Frosty to prevent phones from losing excess battery while the app is running. The issue was that Frosty's sleep timer was only configurable by the user, meaning that if a user never set it, the app could keep running indefinitely and drain the battery. My fix adds a default behavior where Frosty will automatically sleep after 30 minutes of inactivity, even if the user has not interacted with the sleep settings. This way, users who are not aware of the sleep timer feature still get reasonable battery protection out of the box.
