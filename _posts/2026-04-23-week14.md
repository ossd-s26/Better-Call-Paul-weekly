## Group Work on the Project

As we are presenting next week, we have shifted from working on additional PRs into preparing for the presentation. We first created a [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OSRv_MdGiFJNaSGZCbp2TU3YqTK24UXvVkAD4eCi8Ys/edit?tab=t.0) where we laid out the initial presentation format and then each added our own ideas. It was interesting getting to see the intricacies and challenges that each member of my group went through when working through their PRs. I also got a better sense for timelines, as it was easy to assume that we had time to send the PRs since the maintainer was active when we began, but we didn't end up sending anything to him before he stopped being active.

## My Work

Going through the setup of the presentation also gave me a better sense for the work that I did over the course of this project. I now realize that even though I am currently familiar with the project's design and architecture, when I began on that first PR to fix the double play button, I really didn't know anything about it, especially since I was also not familiar with iOS development. So it was great getting to visualize how I progressed.
