## Thoughts on Nanobot Group Report

I thought it was interesting how that group approached working with their community. As the project was created by the HKU Datascience Club, and as a result the maintainers were both students and in a totally separate time zone. They did mention that they used WeChat and Mandarin to communicate so I got a better appreciation for the vital nature of efficient communication when making changes. Also as the project was new and fairly dynamic, I can see how getting into a project at the earlier stages would be beneficial for getting PRs merged quickly as well as being able to work on critical features/bugs. 

## Thoughts on Pandas Group Report

I appreciated how the group went to the new contributors meeting and took notes on the general dynamics in the community. It gave me a sense for how communities are critical even if not every member is actively contributing code. Contributing in terms of steering and media is also important.
